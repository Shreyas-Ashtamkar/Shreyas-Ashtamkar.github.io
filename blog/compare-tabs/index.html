Spec Trajectory

Pick a metric to plot it across generations.

Samsung's inflection was 2023, not incremental. Tab S9 made AMOLED standard on the base model — jumping display score from 6 to 9 in one generation — and never went back. Xiaomi still hasn't made that jump on any base Pad since 2021.

Apple's inflection was chip, not price. A13→A14→A16 across iPad 9/10/11 is a real climb (6→7→8.5), while price actually dropped back to $349 by 2025 after briefly spiking to $449 with the 10th gen redesign. Apple protected value on the base tier hardest of the three.

Xiaomi is the only brand with a flat display line for 4+ years straight. RAM and battery moved, chipset moved modestly, but panel tech (IPS, no OLED) hasn't changed since Pad 5. That's the specific place competitors pulled ahead.

All three brands avoided regression — nobody shipped a worse spec than their prior gen on paper. The competitive gap is about which dimension each brand chose to leave alone.
